Shared Ship (co-op)
[h1]Shared Ship (co-op)[/h1]
[i]Let every player in a Career co-op game command and build on the same ship.[/i]

[h2]Found a bug? Do NOT report it in the comments[/h2]
[b]Report bugs only on GitHub:[/b] https://github.com/UberMorgott/Cosmoteer-Mod-SharedShip/issues

[h2]What it does[/h2]
Mark one or more of your ships as [b]shared[/b], and every human player in the same Career game can:
[list]
[*]command it: move, attack, strafe, direct control;
[*]fire its weapons and pick targets;
[*]manage its crew, resources, toggles and paint;
[*]build, edit and repair on it.
[/list]
The ship also appears in each teammate's own-ship lists (control groups, select own ships, FTL readiness, comms). Ships that are not shared stay 100% vanilla. The ship keeps its real owner: money, upkeep and fleet counts stay with the owner.

[h2]How to use[/h2]
[list]
[*]Select the ship(s), open the ship menu [b]Transfer Ship Control To...[/b] (Career with 2+ players) and pick [b]Everyone (shared ship)[/b]. On a shared ship the entry reads [b]Make private[/b].
[*]Shortcut: [b]Ctrl+Shift+H[/b]. Putting [b][S][/b] anywhere in the ship name does the same.
[*][b]Myself[/b] in the same menu takes the selected ship: a teammate's shared ship becomes your private ship.
[*]Picking a player (vanilla) transfers the ship to them; a shared ship also becomes private.
[*]For teammates' ships to help build or supply it, set [b]Construction Assistance[/b] on those ships to [i]All Allied Ships[/i].
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Yet Another Mod Loader[/b] (Workshop 3577650065), installed with its own instructions.
[*]Enable Shared Ship in the in-game Mods list and [b]trust[/b] its library there.
[*][b]All players must run the exact same version of this mod.[/b] A player without it, or with another version, will desync.
[/list]

[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Making a ship private cancels teammates' direct control only; their other queued orders stay until replaced.
[*]Two players building on the same shared ship at the same moment can override each other's pending edits.
[*]Build and repair costs are paid by the player who orders them.
[*]Crew transfer, resource trade and money stay owner-based (vanilla).
[/list]
